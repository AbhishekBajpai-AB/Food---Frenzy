A LITTLE ABOUT US

Good food brings people together.

FoodFrenzy is a restaurant ordering and management project built to make the everyday food journey feel simple—from discovering a dish to keeping track of an order.

Explore the menu

THE IDEA

A thoughtful experience for both sides of the counter.

Customers can create an account, browse the current menu, place an order, and see its progress. Administrators can manage dishes and stock, look after customer accounts, and move orders through clear preparation steps.

The project brings together a Java backend, a relational database, and server-rendered pages in one approachable application.
